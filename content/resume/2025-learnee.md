Learnee is an online learning platform built on a neuroscience-based
approach. The title said CTO; in practice I covered the entire technical and
visual side of the venture, alongside the founder.

That meant the product end to end: the UX designed in close collaboration
with her, a front-end built with accessibility and performance as
requirements rather than afterthoughts, a Supabase database shaped into an
API tailored to the product's needs, and automated deployment.

It also meant the brand itself: the logo — designed to morph into a friendly
smiley, with its own error face — and every asset around the product, from
LinkedIn communication visuals to video to the website. A deliberately
simple solution, and one of those rare missions where I got to do all of it.

---

Conception et réalisation complète de la plateforme. Mise en place d'une base de
données via Supabase afin de proposer une API adaptée aux besoins spécifiques du
projet. La conception a été menée en étroite collaboration avec la fondatrice.
Développement du frontend en intégrant les enjeux métier, l'UX, l'UI,
l'accessibilité et la performance. Déploiement automatisé mis en place pour
faciliter les livraisons.
